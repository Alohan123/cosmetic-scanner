How accurate is this?

LASERSKIN uses AI to read products and summarize ingredient data. It's a helpful guide — here's how to use it well.

01
Built on live product data

We match each scan against public product pages and ingredient databases, then summarize with AI.

02
An estimate, not a diagnosis

Scores are guidance and can be wrong — especially for new, refilled or look-alike products.

03
Patch-test new actives

Add one active at a time and patch-test first — particularly retinoids, acids and strong exfoliants.

04
For real concerns, see a pro

Persistent or severe skin issues should be reviewed by a dermatologist.
